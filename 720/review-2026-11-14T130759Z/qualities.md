## Qualities checked that pass

- **Speed (Plan Preview):** opening a panel makes no request. It renders from `GeneratedSession.provenance`, already on the payload, and the panel unmounts when closed (Base UI). No new loader call.
- **Speed (session page):** one reader per page, inside the existing `Promise.all`. There is no waterfall and no per-row query. The Catalogue loader now sends the reading instead of pre-worded strings, and the payload size stays about the same.
- **Speed (bundle):** one small shared component and an inline 1-path SVG. No new dependency.
- **Failure:** the empty and absent states hold. A null or unsourced reading renders nothing (no placeholder). Unfilled rows stay visible and are not buttons (route test). Absences, capacity fit and week numbers stay outside the tap. No new outbound call, so no timeout is owed.
- **Access (keyboard and screen reader):** the row is a native `<button>`, `min-h-11` (44px, asserted in e2e), with `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` only while the panel is mounted. The accessible name is the day line plus title, with the separators `aria-hidden` and real spaces between tokens. Enter and Space work natively. Escape closes and returns focus to the row (route tests). Focus outline comes from the base layer, as for `CollapsibleSection`.
- **Access (contrast):** `text-muted-foreground` on the `bg-muted/60` well, computed from the oklch tokens: 5.0:1 light and 6.8:1 dark. On the card it is 5.5:1 light and 7.3:1 dark, all above 4.5:1. The link adds `hover:text-foreground`.
- **Access (reduced motion):** `scrollIntoView` and the hold are instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`. `motion-collapse` keeps its ADR 0077 §5 answer (decided in review run 2). The chevron turn is `motion-safe:`.
- **Security:** the session row is owner-scoped (`getSessionByIdForUser`) before `readPlacedProvenance` sees its Workout id. The non-owned lineage rows select only slot fields and never `takedownReason` (a test asserts its absence). The publisher's name is served only through `isPublished` (ADR 0051 §5), and `readAttribution` whitelists `displayName`/`publishedAt`. The external link is `rel="noreferrer noopener"`, and `locatorHref` only emits `https://doi.org/…` or an `http(s)://` locator from the system corpus. A community row never reaches the locator branch, so no `javascript:` path exists. Nothing secret reaches the client or a log.
- **Insight:** the one silent-null path that means broken data (an `unsourced` placed row) logs `console.warn` with the Catalogue row's and the session Workout's ids (test-pinned), matching the 27 other `console.warn`/`error` calls in `app/utils/*.server.ts`.
- **Data:** no schema change and no migration. The e2e creates and deletes its own retired stock row in `finally`. Nothing seeds in production.
- **UI craft (CLAUDE.md):** press is an instant `active:bg-muted` with no scale (`motion-safe:active:duration-0`). Hover is gated to fine pointers (`@custom-variant hover`). `touch-manipulation` and a transparent tap highlight are set. Text wraps with `[overflow-wrap:anywhere]` plus nbsp tokens. Overflow at 390px is covered by two new e2e cases, and the 320px/200% case still passes per the implement decision. Not verifiable from a read: real-phone feel of the snap-and-hold and of `scrollIntoView` (Safari has no scroll anchoring, so `holdInPlace` does the work there). That belongs in the PR's "needs a real phone" note.
