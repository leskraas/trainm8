# Designer's sealed pick (#587, run 1)

**Pick: In place** — the only variant drawn (unattended run: one direction, the one I would ship).

Why: Nivå og økter is a page the athlete opens on purpose to change their answers, so the
activities are the editor itself (chips pressed as the plan stands), not an offer behind a
closed row as Bytt treningsform is. The preview is the start's own week view laid by the
start's engine for the plan's own family, so it reads the same as the start and Bytt
treningsform, and it is a server answer with one Bekreft under it. It keeps one control per
question (hours and days stay put and are said in one line), and the level/preferences form
keeps its own Lagre below a rule, so the page never has two submits that mean different things
without a heading between them.

What I would hold the critic to watch: the preview is long on a phone (about 2.5 screens in the
demo, 3+ in the worst case), and Bekreft sits at its bottom; I chose not to make it sticky
because a confirm should come after the reading, not float above it.
