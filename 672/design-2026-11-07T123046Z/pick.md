# Designer's pick (sealed until the critic has recommended)

**Pick: Under the card.**

Why: it is the only placement that puts every guard in Plan's lead with no tap and still keeps the lead card a one-glance read. The notices keep the Alert they already have, so their standing does not change. At 390×844 the first notice peeks above the tab bar (about 200 px on demo data, about 127 px in the worst case).

What I would watch: whether that peek is enough on a real phone. If it is not, the next step is a one-line count inside the card that anchors to the stack. I would not move the notices into the card.
