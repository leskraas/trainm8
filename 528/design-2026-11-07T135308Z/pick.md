# Designer's pick (sealed until the critic has recommended)

**Pick: Role steps** (the only variant; unattended run).

Why: mapping by role is what makes the three reading steps appear. A size-for-size sweep would leave
Plan's editors as flat as today, only rounder. The real-app captures show the popovers, the session
page and Meg sitting beside I dag without a seam, and the zone ladder is the only one of the probed
options that holds 3:1 on card in both themes.

What I would watch:
- Decision 7 is the real risk. The light zone values have to change for AC6 to hold, and the spec's
  Scope line says only the dark values change. I would ship the new light values, but only with the
  owner's yes on the one-way door (or a one-line spec fix). The mustard zone 3 is the visible price.
- `PageHeader` at `heading`: on Meg the page then has no `title`-sized text at all. That is right
  for an inline title, but the owner may want the large-title pattern on the tab roots.
- `shadow-md` on Meg's list groups is heavier than today. AC4 asks for it; check it on a phone.
