Decisions

  • How each track's numbers sit on the card: one row per track, the track's name in its ink and a shared name column, replacing today's single comma-joined line of targets.
  • Unit on both figures: mål 32 km · planlagt 24 km, not Sesong's mål 32 km · planlagt 24, because a preview row wraps more often and a screen reader then hears each figure with its unit.
  • No meter in the preview: text and ≠ only. Before approval nothing is done, so Sesong's meter would only draw a dashed outline and a tick, on every track of every week.
  • The page sentence carries the ≠ legend: one footnote under «Sesongen», saying that sessions are placed as published and what ≠ means. Not repeated on the cards.
  • ≠ on a track with nothing placed, in a week with other sessions: show it, by Sesong's week-level rule (hasSessions = the week has sessions). Uke 49's run reads mål 26 km · planlagt 0 km ≠, as Sesong will after approval. (Critic; replaces the per-track rule drawn.)
  • Large text and wrapping: in a narrow container the name stacks above the figures; nowrap only on value + unit; ≠ glued to the last figure; · hung on the target group; no comma before +N uten; subgrid, not display: contents. (Critic.)
  • Hours wording: +1 uten timer (the existing unmeasuredCountLabel), not uten t as criterion 8 writes it. Criterion 3 asks for the existing wording.

Method

  • Tool: Emil's prototype (bredvid-design-engineering prototype-ui), standalone branch, in the tokens from app/styles/tailwind.css (light and dark) and the type steps from docs/design/design-system.md. Unattended run, so one variant.
  • The figures follow the same rules as week-volumes.ts: drift() with DRIFT_TOLERANCE = 0.1, where a week that is short but has unmeasured sessions gets no mark, plus Sesong's hasSessions condition (week-words.ts driftLabel). The words follow WEEK_FIGURE_LABELS, unmeasuredCountLabel, unmeasuredFigureLabel and DRIFT_MARK_LABEL.
  • Today's card: WeekCard in app/routes/training/plan.generate.$eventId.tsx (targets line, ~555–570). The canonical line it follows: TrackMeter in app/routes/_home/timeline/stream.tsx.
  • New label (proposed): PLAN_GENERATE_LABELS.placedAsPublished = «Øktene legges inn slik kilden publiserte dem og skaleres aldri for å treffe ukemålet, så målet og summen av øktene kan være to ulike tall. ≠ betyr at planlagt er mer enn 10 % unna målet.»
  • Screen reader: every figure has its word as visible text (mål, planlagt). ≠ is role="img" with DRIFT_MARK_LABEL, as on Sesong. The list is named «Mål og planlagt per idrett».
  • Motion: none. The figures arrive with the page and change only on a rebuild, which is a navigation (Emil: a static readout does not animate).
  • The rows are unchanged: per-session size is #475's row treatment and out of scope.
  • A fully unfilled track reads planlagt 0 km with no ≠. That is Sesong's rule (a week with nothing written is unwritten, not underplanned), and the unfilled lines below say why.

Rejected directions

  • Sesong's meter row (TrackMeter as is): the closest copy of what comes after approval, but with nothing done it draws only a dashed outline and a tick on every track of up to 30 weeks, and the 72 px meter column pushes the figures onto two lines at 390 px.
  • A gap figure (planlagt 24 km (−8)): this blends the two numbers into a third one, which the criteria forbid.
  • Words only («øktene summerer til mindre enn målet»): rejected by the spec. It hides a number the app already computes.
