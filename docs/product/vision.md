# Vision

> **Draft by `/factory-setup`, for the product owner to correct.** Drawn from
> [`GOAL.md`](../../GOAL.md), `GLOSSARY.md` and the open `wayfinder:map` issues
> (#600, #541, #434). Lines marked "(draft: assumed)" are inferences, not
> statements from those sources. `GOAL.md` stays the fuller statement (pillars,
> non-goals, north-star); this file is the short form the factory proposes from,
> and the two should not drift apart.

**For whom:** the **Self-Coaching Athlete**: an endurance athlete who plans
their own training across run, bike, swim and strength, with no coach, from
beginner to expert.

**The problem:** the plan they follow and the record of what they did live
apart. Strava shows what happened but not against a plan; TrainingPeaks couples
them but turns it into a chore, and building a periodized season by hand asks
the athlete to author a periodization model (#434). Numbers they see are often
estimated or invented, so they can't tell whether to trust them (draft: assumed,
from the building principle in `GOAL.md`).

**The goal:** a training partner that plans forward toward an **Event** (or just
keeps them active), compares every recorded session plainly against the plan,
and adapts what comes next to what they actually did, never showing a fabricated
number. Every metric is earned from real data or shown as an **Unavailable
Metric**.

**Not now:** a social feed, followers or kudos; coach- or team-managed plans;
general-wellness tracking (nutrition, sleep, HRV, weight, mood); an in-app GPS
recorder for cardio (strength set logging is the exception); planning for sports
beyond run, bike, swim and strength; **Shared Training** (named in `GOAL.md`'s
horizon, not current scope).

**Next horizon:**

- **The loop closes**: after real sessions land, the forward plan and the
  **Coach card** adjust to the recorded training state (**Adherence Band**,
  **Training Load**, missed sessions), and say so honestly when the data can't
  justify a change (`GOAL.md` north-star).
- **Review instead of authoring**: say the race, the time and roughly where you
  are, get a complete season with real, cited sessions, then edit by exception
  (#434), on structured workouts read the same way everywhere (#600).
- **Agent-native trainm8**: the athlete's own assistant reads their plan and
  actuals over MCP and proposes changes they answer inside trainm8, with
  training data arriving straight from the watch as FIT (#541) (draft: assumed
  that this ranks after the two above).
