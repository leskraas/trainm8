# Research behind #434 — the plan builder on a phone

Supporting document for [#434](https://github.com/leskraas/trainm8/issues/434), which
is the map. This holds the findings, quotes and file references the map's tickets lean
on; the map holds the route.

Owner's report, verbatim: _"the manual training plan is a bit hard to use, especially on
mobile… maybe it's because I want to have more templates/library/drag and drop stuff."_
Asked which friction was real, the owner named three and **did not** name a fourth:

| Named | Not named |
| --- | --- |
| Lost the overview | Rearranging is fiddly |
| Too much repetition | |
| Don't know what to do next | |

That omission is load-bearing: the one pain drag-and-drop solves is the one pain the
owner does not have.

This is the second pass at this complaint. ADR 0048 was written on the earlier report
that _"making a plan is hard, it is hard to get an overview — especially on a phone"_ and
took the page from 4,284 px to ~2,900 px. The overview problem survived, because page
height was never the cause. The cause is that the surface asks the athlete to author a
periodization model.

> **History note.** This document originally proposed three tracks (mobile fixes →
> catalogue → reuse) under a "make the builder better" framing, with a cut list keyed
> A1–A9 / B1–B12 / C1–C6. That framing was replaced by the generation thesis now on
> #434, and those keys no longer refer to anything. The findings below are unchanged and
> still stand on their own.

## 1. Three positions the research corpus overturned

`c441a77` added ~15,500 lines under `docs/research/`, and `docs/agents/domain.md` gained
a rule that changes how ADRs bind: _"An ADR is a record of a decision, not a constraint
on the next one. Where the evidence says a shipped decision is wrong, recommend the
correct design and name the ADR that should be superseded — don't design around it."_
ADR 0047 is the precedent that a research asset, not only another ADR, can supersede one.
**22 ADRs now carry a Revisit note.**

- **Drag-and-drop is not a rejected idea.** It lost three prototype rounds for #366
  (draggable phase boundaries, a drag-to-sculpt load curve, a drag-cards deck), but the
  capability inventory ranks the calendar grid **#2 of 15** partly because of it:
  _"Planning without drag-to-reschedule is planning people abandon in week three."_ It
  leaves this effort on phone-UX grounds instead — small targets, fights page scroll, no
  undo, and tap-to-move beats it at the same job. Note the winning #366 variant's own
  phone answer was already _"tap-to-arm, tap-to-stamp (phone-native)"_.
- **A workout catalogue is not blocked.** `workout-taxonomy.md` amends ADR 0003: _"The
  1:1 decision is correct and unaffected. But the deferred template library is now the
  binding constraint: an archetype vocabulary without a catalogue to filter is a
  dropdown, not a feature."_
- **Densifying the season chart cannot fix the overview.** `__season-chart.tsx` already
  renders a whole 18-week season at 390 px (~16 px/week) under
  `preserveAspectRatio="none"` — it does not squeeze. It contains **no actuals**: every
  series on it is authored plan. It can only show what was planned, never how the season
  is going. The actual-load colour exists on home (`weekly-build.tsx`, planned ghost vs
  actual fill, coloured by **Adherence Band**) over an 8-week window.

## 2. Why the season overview is a Dashboard zoom and not a calendar route

- **The glossary already picked the shape, and it is not a grid.** `CONTEXT.md:745`
  defines **The Tape** — one horizontal scrubbable timeline, past left, planned right,
  "Now" centred — with _Avoid_: _"Calendar, grid; do not treat as built."_ The
  **Dashboard** entry adds: _"Long-term it is a zoom level of the Tape, not a separate
  concept."_ `GOAL.md:71` has The Tape under Horizon.
- **ADR 0017's Revisit note does not cover this complaint.** It says Supersede — _"Right
  for reading, wrong for planning"_ — but the inventory body qualifies it: _"Its
  reasoning was about **reading** — one mental model, fewer near-empty destinations, no
  duplicate chronological lists — and on that reasoning it was right and remains right.
  Planning is a **writing** surface."_ Losing the overview is a reading complaint.
- **Four of the six capabilities that justify a grid are blocked by something other than
  the surface.** Wellness rows and injury markers are forbidden by `GOAL.md:54` (_"No
  general-wellness tracking"_); weather has no data source; notes days have no model.
  What remains is month rhythm and drag-to-reschedule.
- **Nav has moved on from what ADR 0017 describes.** ADR 0017 §4 put Events and Imports
  in a "More" overflow; that overflow no longer exists — `wordmark-row.tsx` is _"the only
  persistent chrome"_. A new route needs an entry in the home header's wrap row, the most
  width-constrained row in the app. A fourth Dashboard tab costs nothing there:
  `TabsList` is already `max-w-full … overflow-x-auto`.
- **Week-rows, not month-pages.** 390 − 32 px padding, with the `-mx-5/px-5` bleed from
  `week-timeline.tsx` reclaiming the Tile padding, minus six 2 px gaps → **49.4 px per
  cell**, clear of the 44 px floor. At that width the budget is one two-digit day number
  and one load-coloured mark; everything else goes to the inspect panel below, which is
  the house contract (ADR 0030 rule 3, `useChartInspect`). Eighteen weeks is eighteen
  rows ≈ 900 px — one thumb-flick of vertical scroll, no zoom control, no horizontal
  scroll. `grid-cols-7` appears exactly once in the app (`week-timeline.tsx:20`) and is
  gated behind `md:`: the house has never drawn seven columns at phone width.

Costed at **1.5–2.5 days** and ~6 source files, against **4–6 days** plus 3–5 for touch
drag, 10–14 files, and ADR 0017 superseded for the route.

## 3. The catalogue model

**A tier is viewer-relative and derived, never stored.** The same row is *saved* to its
author and *shared* to everyone else, so "community" is a query, not a column:

```ts
type CatalogueTier = 'stock' | 'saved' | 'shared' // derived
tier = authorship === 'system' ? 'stock'
     : ownerId === viewerId    ? 'saved'
     :                           'shared'
```

Three orthogonal axes, only two of which this effort owns:

| Axis | Field | Answers | Owned by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Authorship | `Workout.authorship` + nullable `ownerId` | who wrote it | this effort |
| Membership | existence of a `CatalogueEntry` row | is it offered for reuse | this effort |
| Visibility | `Workout.visibility` (ADR 0037) | who may read it | #337 |

**One Block/Step tree, not two.** `copyWorkout` (`workout.server.ts:214`) already
implements deep-copy-on-instantiate; `WeekPatternDay.workoutId` already points at a
Workout that is deep-copied at stamp (`stamp.server.ts:354`) — template semantics,
shipped. ADR 0003 promised the reuse: _"the Workout model already exists with the correct
structure, and we want to reuse it when templates land."_

**`stock` and `saved` need zero of #337.** Both are owner-scoped or global-read-only and
cross no ownership boundary; the stock read is `WHERE authorship = 'system'`, the same
shape as the shipped `Exercise` catalogue read. The `shared` tier needs four things and
none of #337's mass: a `public` visibility value, a read path that is not owner-scoped
(#337 calls this _"the biggest architectural ripple to chart"_ — today *every* query in
the app is `where: { ownerId: userId }`), a public author identity, and
report-and-takedown.

**Provenance is asymmetric.** A **Stock Workout** carries a structured **Citation**
because trainm8 vouches for what it wrote. A **Shared Workout** carries an
**Attribution** and an explicit non-vouch, never a Citation. A nullable citation field
community authors may fill is the worst outcome — it puts "Daniels 2013" on a session
Daniels never wrote, in the same slot as real authority. Enforce structurally: citation
non-null **only** when `authorship = 'system'`.

**Do not ship a tier that cannot fill.** ADR 0037 is the precedent: it landed a field
with "no consumer, no flow, no UI now" and the field sat unread through an entire map.

## 4. What the research says the seed is gated by

Three existing Revisit notes stand between us and a truthful stock catalogue:

- **ADR 0007 (Amend)** — without `pacePct` and a race-pace kind, _"~⅓ of the running
  library — all of Canova, the whole Norwegian sub-threshold family — is unauthorable."_
- **ADR 0027 A3 (Amend)** — Norwegian session names are content, not chrome; the en-GB
  rule is wrong for _langtur_, _terskel_, _bakkedrag_.
- **ADR 0002 (Amend)** — without `verticalM` and `gradePct`, archetypes H and G cannot be
  seeded.

Per `workout-taxonomy.md:983`: _"Do not let the app generate a session named after a
protocol it did not reproduce. A named protocol carries its intensity anchor or it is not
that protocol."_ Seeding a lactate-anchored protocol as a pace session is the failure
`intensityFidelity` exists to prevent.

## 5. Findings that argue for generation specifically

- `workouts-running.md` §13.1: _"**The manual planner (ADR 0039) gets a starting point.**
  'Add a threshold session' resolves to eight cited candidates rather than an empty
  form."_
- Inventory 6.7, Week patterns / stamping, rated **Have**: _"Building 20 weeks by hand is
  the reason people quit planning."_
- Inventory 6.2, Workout library, **Partial**: _"Nobody re-authors '4×8 min threshold'
  every week."_
- `workouts-running.md:552`: _"Plan generation becomes retrieval-and-substitute over a
  cited corpus… it makes generation provenance real rather than nominal."_
- **`GOAL.md` pillar 2 already names generation the engine**: _"Plan Generation, the Plan
  Outline, and the Coach card's daily 'go hard or recover?' call are the engine that feeds
  the loop."_ The thesis needs no loosening of the north star. Pillar 3 cuts the other
  way and belongs in the argument about what survives: _"Meets you at your level — enough
  for a beginner, **everything for an expert**."_

## 6. Constraints any surface on this route must hold

- **ADR 0028** — the phone (390×844) is the reference viewport; 44 px controls;
  `text-base md:text-sm`; every screen change driven with Playwright before its ticket
  closes.
- **`/training/plan` is inside the overflow guard** — `tests/e2e/mobile-overflow.test.ts`
  asserts `scrollWidth - clientWidth <= 0` at 390 px on both readings. Any horizontal
  strip needs its own `overflow-x` container; `<html>` carries `overflow-x-hidden`, so a
  wide child clips rather than scrolls.
- **ADR 0030 rule 3** — tap-to-inspect into a fixed panel below, never a floating
  tooltip. There is no hover on touch.
- **Guards are never behind progressive disclosure** (#429) — _"a warning withheld is a
  warning broken."_
- **Every derived figure keeps its `authored | derived` marker** with the derivation one
  interaction away, and an **Unavailable Metric** renders as unavailable rather than a
  fabricated zero.
